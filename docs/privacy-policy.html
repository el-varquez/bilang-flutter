Privacy Policy

Bilang — listed on Google Play as “Inventory Scanner”. Last updated 2 September 2026.

The short version

Everything stays on your phone. Bilang has no account, no login, no cloud, and no analytics. It collects nothing, shares nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.

Camera

Bilang uses the camera for one thing: reading barcodes on the device, using Google’s ML Kit running locally. Camera frames are processed in memory and never stored, and never leave the phone.

Your data

Your stock counts (item names, barcodes, quantities) are stored only in the app’s local storage on your device. Deleting a count, or uninstalling the app, deletes them. We have no copy — we never see them.

Export

When you export a count, the file goes wherever you choose to share or save it — that is entirely in your hands. Bilang operates no server of its own and makes no network connections.

Permissions

Camera — barcode scanning, as above. No other permissions are requested.

Children

Bilang is a stock-counting utility intended for general audiences. It collects no data from anyone, children included.
