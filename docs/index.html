Inventory Scanner

Scan a barcode. It counts.

A stock-counting app for any store — your phone works like a hardware inventory terminal. Fully offline: no account, no cloud, no ads.

Three ways to scan

The camera reads one barcode per press. A Bluetooth or USB scanner is always live — no field to tap. And a barcode that won't scan can simply be typed.

Fix it on the row

Steppers nudge a quantity, tapping it types an exact number, a swipe deletes the row. Counting shrink-wrapped packs? Batch scan makes one scan add 24.

Export anywhere

Every count exports as CSV, Excel, or JSON — three columns, name · barcode · qty — a file any POS or spreadsheet can read. Share it to any app on your phone.

Private by design

Your counts live on your phone and nowhere else. Bilang collects nothing, sends nothing, and makes no network connections — the only thing that ever leaves the device is the export file you choose to share.
